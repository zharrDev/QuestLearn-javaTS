QuestLearn Typing - Demo Build
===============================

Run:
  QuestLearnTyping.exe

Modes:
  - Guest: play immediately, no backend required. Progress is saved locally.
  - Account: sign in with siswa@demo.id / demo123 when the Quarkus backend
    is running at http://localhost:8080/api.

Controls:
  - Letters / numbers / space: type the highlighted letter.
  - Enter: start a run; from the result screen, play again.
  - Esc: pause/resume; go back from menus.
  - Mouse: menus, SFX mute, pause.

Tips:
  - Clear the whole word before its cargo reaches the hero.
  - Accented letters can be typed without accents.
  - The settings screen supports reduced motion, screen-shake toggle, and a
    colourblind palette.

Notes:
  - This is the university demo build. Music is not implemented yet.
  - Placeholder art and font licences are listed in CREDITS.txt.

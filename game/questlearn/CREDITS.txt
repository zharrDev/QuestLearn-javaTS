QuestLearn Typing
=================

Game, UI, and gameplay code: QuestLearn team
Engine: Godot 4.3 (https://godotengine.org)
Internal resolution: 640x360, nearest-neighbour scaling

Placeholder art
---------------
Backgrounds, crates, and hero frames originate from the QuestLearn placeholder
asset pack in frontend/public/assets. Original source and licence are pending
confirmation; see ASSET_LICENSES.md before public distribution.

Procedural work
---------------
Cliffs, ground, ambient particles, HUD frames, scanlines, vignette, slash arcs,
floating text, slider styling, and runtime-synthesised SFX are original project
work.

Demo note
---------
This build is intended for the university demo/expo. Music is not implemented.
The backend is a development configuration (H2, open CORS, demo JWT secret).
